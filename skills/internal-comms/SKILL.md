# internal-comms

Lead with the answer/decision, then context. Structures:
- Status: what shipped, what's next, risks/blockers, asks.
- Incident: impact, timeline, root cause, fix, prevention.
- Update: TL;DR, details, decisions needed.
Keep it skimmable — headers, short paragraphs, bullets. State owners and dates.
